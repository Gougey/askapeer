Search vocabulary and tags — decided

For: Andrew to review · Drafted: 21 October 2026 · Revised: 7 November 2026 · Status: all decided, nothing further needed

1. Abbreviations: decided

Thank you. Your answers on all 36 rows are recorded, and nothing further is needed here.

  • In: MET, EMS, GPS, SLR and S&C. Typing S&C, S+C or S and C now searches for “strength and conditioning”.
  • Out: the other 25 short forms, plus WHAT and OSPRO.
  • Combined terms already work. Search needs every word you type, so Tinel’s sign foot finds 13 papers, pivot shift knee 2,239, Nordic hamstring 278 and elbow bursa 57. Tinel’s sign, pivot shift and Nordic therefore need no links, and you don’t need to add a list of bursae for search.

2. Tags: decided

Thank you. All your tag answers are now in the app.

  • Labels: every tag that shares a name now shows which one it is, such as Foot (muscles) and Sacrum (bone), on discussion cards and in the tag picker.
  • Areas: picking Foot, Thigh, Forearm, Sacrum, Ilium, Ischium, Coccyx, Pubis or Acetabulum searches everything under that name.
  • Merged: the acromioclavicular, coracoacromial and coracohumeral ligaments under Shoulder girdle, and Adductor tendinopathy under Groin.
  • Tidied: Plantar plate sits under the metatarsophalangeal joints only. Sacrum, Ilium, Ischium and Coccyx lost the repeat of themselves. Joint Disorders has gone, with its conditions moved up a level.
  • Added: Facet arthropathy in the thoracic section, and your twelve muscles under Inguinal region, including a new Iliopsoas tag.
  • Renamed: Ligamentum flavum, with the plural kept as a synonym.
  • Osteoporotic fracture: one tag beside Osteoporosis, which also covers papers saying “osteoporosis-related fracture”.

3. Nothing for you to do

  • Two links added from your testing: shin splints ↔︎ medial tibial stress syndrome and hip replacement ↔︎ hip arthroplasty, each working both ways. If other common names and formal terms should be linked like this, please add them to your reply.
  • Three draft rows dropped as already covered: RTS → return-to-sport programme and return-to-sport rehabilitation, and ROM → range-of-motion exercise. RTS is already linked to “return to sport” and ROM to “range of motion”, which find those papers too.
  • UK and US spelling (paediatric ↔︎ pediatric, tumour ↔︎ tumor, randomised ↔︎ randomized): agreed in the draft, not built yet. It comes next and needs nothing from you.
