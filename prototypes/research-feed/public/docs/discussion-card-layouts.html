Decisions this needs, beyond the layout

  1. The case cue — to be resolved. A 1px teal border on its own fails the accessibility rule the app is built to (WCAG 2.2, style guide §9.2): it is invisible to some colour-blind readers and faint on any screen. Before this is built it needs a second cue alongside the colour — a short "Case" label before the handle is the simplest.
  2. Gold for both awards. Kudos gold is the app's one status colour; the two awards are both kudos, so both are gold when awarded and grey at zero. That is a deliberate amendment to the style guide (§2.1 currently names the star).
  3. Where you award. Today the card only shows kudos — you award inside the discussion. Recommended to keep it so: tappable icons on a card that is itself one big link are easy to mis-tap on a phone.
  4. How answers are ranked. Answers are ordered by kudos now. With two awards: by their sum (simplest), or let the reader choose.
  5. Existing kudos. Today's stars can't be split after the fact. For the pilot the clear-down empties them anyway, so this is free to decide now.
