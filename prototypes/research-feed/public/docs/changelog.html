What’s new in Askapeer

For: the pilot group and the team · Each release has a version number, shown at the foot of the Profile screen (“Askapeer 0.9.0 · build a3f30f4 · 12 Nov 2026”). If something looks wrong, quoting that line tells us exactly what you are running.

0.9.0 — 12 November 2026: the pilot

The first version real members use. Everything below was built and tested before the pilot began; this is the starting line rather than a list of changes.

  • Invitation-only registration. Only invited email addresses can register, and an invited colleague is approved on registering.
  • Sign in with a six-digit code emailed to you, which also works in the app added to your home screen.
  • Pseudonymous handles. Your handle is the only name anyone sees. Your real name and registration number are never shown.
  • Discussions: ask a question or post a de-identified case, answer, and follow a discussion to hear when it moves. Search at the top of the page by keyword, tag, type and sort.
  • Two awards for answers: Insightful and Useful. A good question shows in how many people follow it.
  • My Research: over 300,000 sports medicine and physiotherapy papers, searched by keyword, type of paper and period, with clinical shorthand understood (rehab, ACL, S&C) and spelling corrected. Save any paper to come back to.
  • Tags from Andrew’s clinical vocabulary, labelled where two share a name, such as Foot (muscles) and Foot (conditions).
