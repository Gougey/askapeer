# Search abbreviations and spellings — draft for Andrew

**For:** Andrew to review · **Drafted:** 21 October 2026 · **Status:** draft, not built

Andrew searched *Achilles rehab* and got 8 papers; *Achilles rehabilitation* found 721. Search matches words by their root, and "rehab" has a different root from "rehabilitation", so the two never meet. The same happens with *physio* (393 papers) and *physiotherapy* (75,007), and with almost every abbreviation clinicians use.

This list fixes that. Each row links a short form to its full term, so searching either finds papers that use either. It applies to the Research search and to Discussions.

**How to review:** every row has a proposed decision. You only need to tell us what you would change:

- **✓ Include**: we will add it unless you say otherwise.
- **? Check**: the short form also means something else, so including it would bring in unrelated papers. Tell us include or leave out.
- **Anything missing**: add the shorthand you and your colleagues actually type. Your Part 3 list gave us most of this, and the everyday shorthand at the top is our own first guess.

The numbers come from the 180,000-odd papers on live today. *"700 → 42,402"* means typing that form finds 700 papers now and would find 42,402 with the list.

**Totals:** 324 pairs — 291 proposed to include, 33 to check.

## 1. UK and US spelling — a rule, not a list

The literature is mostly written in US spelling, so a search in UK spelling misses most of it:

| You type | Finds | US spelling | Finds |
|---|---|---|---|
| paediatric | 1,566 | pediatric | 5,508 |
| oedema | 579 | edema | 2,563 |
| randomised | 6,835 | randomized | 37,355 |
| tumour | 757 | tumor | 3,492 |
| haemarthrosis | 65 | hemarthrosis | 224 |
| programme | 6,637 | program | 26,703 |
| behaviour | 3,799 | behavior | 7,752 |

Rather than listing every word, the search will apply the standard spelling rules both ways: *ae* and *oe* ↔ *e* (paediatric, oedema), *-our* ↔ *-or* (tumour), *-ise / -isation* ↔ *-ize / -ization* (randomised), and *-tre* ↔ *-ter* (centre). **Nothing for you to review here**, unless you know of medical words where the rule would be wrong.
