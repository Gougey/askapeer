# Search vocabulary and tags — what still needs your decision

**For:** Andrew to review · **Drafted:** 21 October 2026 · **Revised:** 7 November 2026 · **Status:** the abbreviations are decided; six tag questions are waiting for you

## 1. Abbreviations: decided

Thank you. Your answers on all 36 rows are recorded, and nothing further is needed here.

- **In:** MET, EMS, GPS, SLR and S&C. Typing S&C, S+C or S and C now searches for “strength and conditioning”.
- **Out:** the other 25 short forms, plus WHAT and OSPRO.
- **Combined terms already work.** Search needs every word you type, so *Tinel’s sign foot* finds 13 papers, *pivot shift knee* 2,239, *Nordic hamstring* 278 and *elbow bursa* 57. Tinel’s sign, pivot shift and Nordic therefore need no links, and you don’t need to add a list of bursae for search.

## 2. Tags that share a name

79 tag names are used by more than one tag, 178 tags in all. They look identical on a discussion card but are separate tags, so someone who picks one Foot tag won't find a discussion tagged with the other. Adrian hit the same kind of mismatch with Knee Joint and Knee joints.

**What we propose:** add a short label to each, shown only on discussion cards and in the tag picker, such as *Foot (muscles)* and *Foot (conditions)*. The tags themselves don't change, so how papers are matched stays exactly the same. The labels make the choice clear, but the tags stay separate: a discussion tagged *Foot (conditions)* still won't appear under *Foot (muscles)*.

**Agreed so far:** your labels stand, apart from your changes. Plantar plate moves to under the metatarsophalangeal joint only, Facet arthropathy is added to the thoracic section, and Joint Disorders is removed, with what sits beneath it moved up a level so nothing is lost. For Ligamenta flava we suggest **Ligamentum flavum**, which finds 212 papers against 4 today, with the plural kept as a synonym.

**Six questions:**

1. **Acromioclavicular ligament:** merge into the copy under Shoulder girdle? Coracoacromial and coracohumeral ligaments are split the same way. Should they merge too?
2. **Adductor tendinopathy:** should the merged tag sit under Groin or Hip?
3. **Sacrum:** there are four copies and you named two (bone, sacroiliac joint). What should happen to the second bones copy, and to the conditions heading with 9 items under it?
4. **Coccyx, Ilium and Ischium:** two copies of each share a label. What wording would you like, or should they merge?
5. **Inguinal region:** which muscles should go under it?
6. **Osteoporotic fracture:** where should it sit? It is under lumbar and thoracic today and is applied to 500 papers wherever the fracture is. A lower-limb copy would need a rule tying each copy to its body area, or it would be applied to the same papers again.

Labels can go in straight away. Merging is a bigger taxonomy change that we would plan separately, so any you mark *merge* use the proposed label in the meantime.
