## 1. Abbreviations: decided

## 2. Tags: decided

Thank you. All your tag answers are now in the app.

- **Labels:** every tag that shares a name now shows which one it is, such as *Foot (muscles)* and *Sacrum (bone)*, on discussion cards and in the tag picker.
- **Areas:** picking Foot, Thigh, Forearm, Sacrum, Ilium, Ischium, Coccyx, Pubis or Acetabulum searches everything under that name.
- **Merged:** the acromioclavicular, coracoacromial and coracohumeral ligaments under Shoulder girdle, and Adductor tendinopathy under Groin.
- **Tidied:** Plantar plate sits under the metatarsophalangeal joints only. Sacrum, Ilium, Ischium and Coccyx lost the repeat of themselves. Joint Disorders has gone, with its conditions moved up a level.
- **Added:** Facet arthropathy in the thoracic section, and your twelve muscles under Inguinal region, including a new Iliopsoas tag.
- **Renamed:** Ligamentum flavum, with the plural kept as a synonym.
- **Osteoporotic fracture:** one tag beside Osteoporosis, which also covers papers saying “osteoporosis-related fracture”.
