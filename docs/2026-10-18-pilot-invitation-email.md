# Pilot — invitation email (draft)

**For:** Andrew to send to each colleague, from his own email address · **Status:** draft for review

Sent personally rather than by the platform, for two reasons. A message from a colleague is far less likely to be filtered than one from a new domain. And it is Andrew's endorsement that makes the invitation mean something.

**Before sending:** each colleague's address must already be on the invitation list. Registration refuses any address that is not, and they must register with the exact address this email was sent to.

---

**Subject:** An invitation to try Askapeer

Hi [first name],

I'd like to invite you to try Askapeer, a new network I've been helping to build for registered sports medicine practitioners.

The idea is simple. Everyone on it is verified as a registered professional, but everyone is anonymous to each other. Nobody knows whether you're a consultant of thirty years or six months out of your degree, so a question or an idea is judged on its merit, not on who asked it. You can ask the things you'd never ask in a corridor, and disagree with anyone.

We're running a small pilot with about fifteen colleagues, and I'd value your honest opinion of it.

**Getting started takes about five minutes.** There's a short guide here, with screenshots:

https://askapeer-web.fly.dev/welcome

In short:

1. Register at the link above, **using this email address** — registration is by invitation only, and it is how we know you were invited.
2. We'll email you a six-digit code to sign in. If it hasn't arrived within a couple of minutes, check your junk or quarantine folder for mail from no-reply@mail.askapeer.co.uk.
3. Choose a handle — the only name anyone will ever see. Your real name and registration number are never shown to other members.
4. Add it to your phone's home screen so it opens like an app. The guide shows how on iPhone and Android.

One thing matters above all: Askapeer only works because nobody knows who anyone is. Please don't reveal who you are, or try to work out who anyone else is — it means permanent removal, without exception.

If anything is confusing, broken or missing, just reply to this email. That feedback is exactly what the pilot is for.

Thanks — I hope you find it useful.

Andy

## Notes for review

- **The link** is the current live address. If a custom domain is in place before the invitations go out, update it here and in the welcome guide.
- **Screenshots inline.** The welcome guide carries them, so the email stays short and is less likely to be filtered — emails heavy with images are a common spam signal. If Andrew would rather include one, the registration form (`apps/web/public/guide/register.png`) is the most useful.
- **Reply-to.** Replies go to Andrew. If feedback should reach Adrian too, Andrew can copy him in, or forward.
