The footer, line by line

Line 1
Handle, then the author's Insightful and Useful totals — all in grey
Line 2
Date on the left; this post's Insightful, Useful, Answers and Following on the right, each in its own colour
  • Line 1 is the person; line 2 is this post. The date moved down because it belongs to the question, not to the author, as you suggested.
  • Grey says "the person", colour says "this post". The author's totals use the same two icons, but grey and slightly smaller; the post's are in colour. A zero is grey wherever it appears.
  • Everything fits on a phone. With handles at 20 characters, the longest handle plus three-digit totals fits on line one (the case card above), and line two has room to spare even with a full date.
  • Four colours, all readable. Insightful violet, Useful amber, Answers blue, Following magenta. Each passes the WCAG AA contrast needed for small text, in light and dark — and each also has its own icon, so nothing depends on colour alone.
  • Teal stays reserved for the clinical-case border, and red for the Ask button, so neither is used for a count.

Decided, 20 October

  1. Clinical case: a small teal cross before the title, as well as the teal border. Screen readers hear "Clinical case:" before the title.
  2. Awarding happens inside the discussion, with the icon and the word on each button (below). The card only shows the counts.
  3. Answers are ranked by Insightful and Useful combined.
  4. Every count is always shown — grey at zero, in its colour once it is above zero. That includes Following.
  5. The style guide's kudos rules are rewritten to match: four stat colours, gold no longer the single status colour.
  6. Handles are 20 characters at most for new handles; the pilot clear-down removes every existing one.
