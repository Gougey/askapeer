The living companion to STYLE_GUIDE.md, rendered from the real design tokens. Three rules override all else — never introduce a hierarchy signal, show anonymity, and make patient safety a component, not a policy page. Headlines are set in Fraunces; text and UI in Inter. Toggle the theme, top-right.

01 — Foundations

Colour

A restrained palette on a large neutral canvas. Kudos gold is the product's one status colour and is never used for anything else. Spark red is rationed to the Ask action and the wordmark.

Brand

Neutrals

Accessibility — text contrast on surface (WCAG 2.2 AA · normal text ≥ 4.5:1)

Raw kudos gold fails as text — use --color-kudos-text for words, keeping the gold for the filled star + count (a graphical pairing). --color-faint is decoration / large text only.

Typography

Fraunces — an "old-style" serif with optical warmth — carries every heading, giving AskaPeer editorial authority (the WHO register) and a premium, timeless feel (the Rolex register). Inter carries body, UI and data: crisp, neutral, highly legible. British English throughout.

Serif is for headings only. Never set body, labels, buttons or data in the serif — the sans keeps the interface clinical and fast to read.

Spacing, radius & elevation

A 4px base unit; 16px is the workhorse. Radii run from 12px inputs to the 999px pill; monogram tiles are rounded squares, never circles.

What changed in v0.2

• Fraunces serif on the screen title, card headings and the thread question — instant editorial authority.
• Inter for everything else — crisper and more designed than the old system default.
• A short accent keyline under the screen title — a WHO-style editorial signature (one per screen).
• More generous spacing, hairline section rhythm, and a confident type scale.
• The palette, components and safety patterns are unchanged — this is a theming pass, not a redesign.
