A AskaPeer Design System · v0.1

The living companion to STYLE_GUIDE.md. Everything here is rendered from the real design tokens. AskaPeer is the no-ego sports medicine network: verified, pseudonymous, merit over rank. Three rules override all else — never introduce a hierarchy signal, show anonymity, and make patient safety a component, not a policy page. Toggle the theme, top-right.

01 — Foundations

Colour

A restrained palette on a large neutral canvas. Kudos gold is the product's one status colour and is never used for anything else. Spark red is rationed to the Ask action and the wordmark.

Brand

Neutrals

Accessibility — text contrast on surface (WCAG 2.2 AA · normal text needs ≥ 4.5:1)

Raw kudos gold fails as text — use --color-kudos-text for words, and keep the gold for the filled star + count (a graphical pairing). --color-faint is decoration / large text only.

Typography

The native system font stack — instant, familiar, no personality to fight the content. British English throughout; sentence case except the wordmark and micro-labels.

Spacing, radius & elevation

A 4px base unit; 16px is the workhorse (screen margins and card padding). Radii run from 12px inputs to the 999px pill; monogram tiles are rounded squares, never circles.
